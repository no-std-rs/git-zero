## Exact production policy

MULTI retains an insertion-ordered Vec of at most32 independently verified immutable chunks: one anchor and at most31 secondaries. ONE is the same bounded mechanism with one slot; `off|on|one|multi` are generic production controls, `on` aliases ONE, and the candidate defaults to MULTI on cold Cloudflare hosts. Warm hosts retain no chunk pool. Admission sees every already-required verified ordinary GET miss in selected order, with no first32-candidate truncation. It never evicts a live chunk or response to admit a later item. PUT/import, startup/recovery, observer/stats and planning paths cannot admit.

The **actual owning32MiB joint budget** and256 combined response/chunk entries always apply. Each candidate payload must be <=16MiB. In addition, when the pool is nonempty, total computed chunk ownership for a later admission must be <=**17MiB (17,825,792B)**:32MiB minus an **unallocated15MiB (15,728,640B) soft headroom preference**. This is a chunk-admission ceiling, not a claim that those15MiB exist as retained allocations or that actual control needs equal1MiB. The empty-pool anchor is exempt from this secondary heuristic but still must fit actual joint ownership. The chunk calculation includes payload/key capacities, private data/Arc/header charge, pool Vec capacity/header allowance, and conservative old+new backing ownership during growth. Consequently a candidate may be rejected even when a simpler payload-only model would admit it. No optimality or model-count equivalence is asserted.

Actual ownership includes response key/header/body and ring capacity, pool backing capacity even after nonempty pruning, certificates/history, pending publication/lineage/advertisement/proof state and the approved conditional262,144B socket reservation. Empty clear/disable/error paths release the pool backing; nonempty capacity remains charged. Required response/control allocations reclaim newest secondaries, then the anchor, before response LRU reclamation or safe failure. Response prechecks exclude reclaimable chunk ownership: otherwise eligible responses through the existing16MiB limit remain retainable. No14MiB response cap or fictitious idle reservation exists. `cache_bytes/cache_entries` remain response-only; chunk payload/owning/entry gauges and joint current/high-water explain surviving chunks.32MiB bounds this retained cache domain, not total process/request-local memory.

Pressure diagnostics also count explicit mode-limit reductions. All runtime gates set mode before population, so their pressure deltas reflect actual required ownership/entry pressure. Reset/error evictions and confirmed prunes have separate counters. Socket diagnostics advertise a constant bound even when transport is disabled; actual `socket_storage_bytes()` charges its reservation conditionally.
