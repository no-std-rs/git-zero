# Independent cache-headroom design audit

**A credible material gap remains: prefer one bounded test of actual-joint-only admission before the narrower fresh-bootstrap design.** The source contains an unallocated 15 MiB admission heuristic that rejects useful chunks while the real 32 MiB budget has room. A fresh R18 readset replay, including response pressure and current Full pruning, yields **193 additional conditional GETs avoided / $0.06948** beyond the current MULTI heuristic per 1,000 four-profile repetitions. This is a conditional design result, not an R19 score, source approval, winner or stopping bound. No positive unconditional net saving is proved.

## The source-proven gap and one concrete policy

Pinned R19 `f124d3b71ce1acc44ab20d6850b2b619b4bd8ec5`, `native.rs:1873–1919`, admits the first resident anchor under actual joint ownership. Every later candidate additionally requires `chunk_needed <= 32 MiB - 15 MiB`. That is a **17 MiB ceiling on all chunk ownership**, including the anchor, pool slots, headers and an old/new backing-growth overlap. The 15 MiB is unallocated; the implementation correctly does not claim it as retained memory or impose a 14 MiB response limit. The additional rejection condition is therefore an admission choice, separate from actual budget safety.

Recommend **actual joint-budget first-fit**: remove only that nonempty-pool headroom predicate. Keep the existing actual `non_chunk_bytes + chunk_needed <= 32 MiB` check, at most 32 chunk entries inside the existing 256 combined entries, exact verified payload/key/capacity/Arc/header ownership, and the same old/new Vec backing charge. Keep the 16 MiB response-entry eligibility limit. Reject a miss that does not fit; never evict a live chunk merely to admit a later scan item. This needs no new predictor, response high-water state, partition, allocation or storage format.

Keep R19's real-pressure order: newest secondary, then older residents through the anchor, then response LRU. Required response insertion, socket/certificate/control growth and pending-publication ownership take priority and are charged before allocation. Preserve the exact current Full confirmed-prune predicate and promotion behavior. No projection subset, proposed manifest, observer-only reconstruction or ambiguous publication may broaden pruning. Admit every qualifying already-required successful exact verified public-Git GET miss, never PUT, startup, observer, stats or warm setup bytes. The policy uses no digest, fixture, generation, operation-group or history oracle.

For attribution and review, use one generic production policy control to compare **same-source current MULTI headroom versus actual-joint MULTI**, with the same unchanged Worker. The first gate must not compare against ONE alone: that would mix R19's already implemented pool opportunity with the headroom change. Exact flag/enum naming is an implementation choice after design approval.
