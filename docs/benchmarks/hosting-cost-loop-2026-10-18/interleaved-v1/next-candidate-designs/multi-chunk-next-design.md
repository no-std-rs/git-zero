## One concrete production policy

Retain the first verified ordinary-public-Git GET chunk as ONE's anchor, capped at16MiB. Add a **first-fit protected secondary pool**, at most31 additional immutable chunks. Admit only an already-required GET whose entire owning allocation fits; a live cached entry is never evicted merely to admit a later item in a scan. After confirmed publication, remove entries no longer referenced by that authoritative manifest; never infer successful retirement from an attempted CAS. A stale or failed publication must not prune the current generation's entries. Reads still authenticate current generation/manifest, exact key/length and ordered reconstruction. No admission from PUT, setup, metrics/stats, warmup work outside ordinary public operations, or speculative/dummy GET.

Use one fixed common partition of the existing32MiB budget: **14MiB for response ownership,256KiB for R17 socket ownership,1MiB for other control/slot metadata**, with the remaining bytes available to anchor plus secondary payload. All exact allocations and headers must be charged; the replay provisionally charges256bytes per secondary slot inside its pool. At most32 chunk entries count within the existing256 total entries; responses/certificates remain inside that same ceiling. This raises no cap. A response too large for its retained share may be sent but not retained; that generic tradeoff needs the normal guards.

With the actual anchor sizes8,544,710/16,777,216 bytes, secondary serialized-payload ceilings before per-slot charge are **9,018,938 /786,432 bytes**. The first accommodates two early~4.37MiB compaction outputs plus small commit chunks. Keep residents protected until loss of reachability or real budget pressure, rather than ordinary FIFO/LRU replacement on every miss. Pruning the21 retired small chunks per cold trace recovers room for later small chunks; retaining them indefinitely wastes the limited pool.
